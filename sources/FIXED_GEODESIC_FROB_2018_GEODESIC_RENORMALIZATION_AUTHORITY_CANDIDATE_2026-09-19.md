## Exact source relevance

Fröb studies a diffeomorphism-invariant scalar two-point observable whose second point is fixed by a geodesic of prescribed length/direction.

When the geodesic is expanded perturbatively, graviton fields and their derivatives are integrated along the one-dimensional background geodesic.

The source explicitly explains the resulting UV issue:

- the relevant graviton objects are distributions;
- restricting such distributions to the one-dimensional geodesic is not generically defined;
- this creates new divergences which are not removed by the usual bulk counterterms alone.

The paper introduces a separate renormalization of the geodesic embedding coordinates,

`chi^mu(tau) -> Z_chi chi^mu(tau)`,

with

`Z_chi = 1 + delta Z_chi`,

and derives a one-loop divergent counterterm of the schematic dimensional form

`delta Z_chi = zeta kappa^2 / ell^2 + O(kappa^4)`.

The renormalized fixed-geodesic scalar two-point observable is then finite and gauge independent in the stated four-dimensional one-loop setting.
