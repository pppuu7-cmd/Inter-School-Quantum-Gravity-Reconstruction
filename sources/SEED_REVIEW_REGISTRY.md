## SRC-001 — Spinfoams / LQG

Etera R. Livine, **Spinfoam Models for Quantum Gravity: Overview** (2024).  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09364

Seed claims to audit in concrete realizations:
- spinfoams formulate a path-integral/state-sum over quantized discrete spacetime geometries;
- the framework sits at the intersection of LQG, Regge calculus, dynamical triangulations and TQFT ideas;
- EPRL is a central four-dimensional realization;
- transition amplitudes and semiclassical/continuum interpretation are key interface objects.

Evidence class: `SOURCE_GROUNDED_REVIEW / REALIZATION_AUDIT_REQUIRED`.

## SRC-002 — Spinfoam foundations

Jonathan Engle, Simone Speziale, **Spinfoams: Foundations** (2023).  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.20147

Seed claims:
- spinfoams aim to provide covariant dynamics for LQG and a physical inner product/projector associated with constraints;
- the semiclassical relation to Regge-calculus path integrals is a key recovery channel;
- the EPRL model is a principal concrete realization.

Evidence class: `SOURCE_GROUNDED_REVIEW / REALIZATION_AUDIT_REQUIRED`.

## SRC-003 — Causal Dynamical Triangulations

J. Ambjørn, R. Loll, **Causal Dynamical Triangulations: Gateway to Nonperturbative Quantum Gravity** (2024).  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09399

Seed claims:
- CDT implements a nonperturbative background-independent Lorentzian gravitational path integral on dynamical lattices;
- diffeomorphism-invariant observables and Planck-regime quantitative results can be studied numerically;
- emergence of a de Sitter-like universe and short-distance spectral-dimension behavior are central reported results.

Evidence class: `SOURCE_GROUNDED_REVIEW / REALIZATION_AUDIT_REQUIRED`.

## SRC-004 — Asymptotic safety

Astrid Eichhorn, **Asymptotically safe quantum gravity and its phenomenology — a review** (2026).  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21522

Seed claims:
- asymptotic safety treats gravity within quantum field theory and seeks UV completion through quantum scale symmetry/nontrivial fixed-point behavior;
- current work includes matter sectors and increasingly Lorentzian formulations;
- connections between approaches and universal features are explicitly identified as an important direction.

Evidence class: `SOURCE_GROUNDED_REVIEW / TRUNCATION_AND_Lorentzian_SCOPE_AUDIT_REQUIRED`.

## SRC-005 — Causal sets

Sumati Surya, **The causal set approach to quantum gravity** (2019).  
https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11544

Seed claims:
- causal set theory replaces continuum spacetime with a locally finite partially ordered set;
- order represents proto-causality and local finiteness encodes discreteness;
- Lorentzian causality is fundamental and characteristic nonlocality can arise alongside continuum Lorentz invariance.

Evidence class: `SOURCE_GROUNDED_REVIEW / DYNAMICS_REALIZATION_AUDIT_REQUIRED`.

## SRC-006 — Holography / quantum information / emergent spacetime

Tadashi Takayanagi, **Emergent Holographic Spacetime from Quantum Information** (2025).  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06595

Seed claims:
- holographic duality relates gravitational theories to quantum many-body systems;
- quantum-information quantities, especially entanglement structures, provide direct links between microscopic quantum systems and emergent gravitational geometry;
- extension beyond standard holographic settings and emergence of time remain open directions.

Evidence class: `SOURCE_GROUNDED_ESSAY_REVIEW / CONCRETE_DUALITY_REALIZATION_REQUIRED`.
