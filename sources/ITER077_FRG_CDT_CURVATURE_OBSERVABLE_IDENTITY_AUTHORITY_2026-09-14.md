## A — CDT quantum-curvature definition

**PASS.**

The 2020 CDT source defines quantum Ricci curvature operationally through a quasi-local metric construction. For two geodesic spheres of radius `delta` whose centers are a distance `delta` apart, it computes the normalized average pairwise distance between sphere points and writes the dimensionless curvature observable through the deviation of

`d_bar(S_p^delta,S_p'^delta) / delta`

from its flat/reference behavior.

The construction uses only source-defined distance and volume data and therefore applies directly to the piecewise-flat CDT ensemble. The probe scale is the geodesic radius `delta`, in lattice/metric distance units, not CDT proper time and not an RG scale.

The source measures expectation values on full four-dimensional CDT configurations and explicitly discusses directional dependence, lattice artifacts, non-universal normalization and ensemble averaging.

## B — FRG curvature-object definition

**PASS, BUT AS A DIFFERENT OBJECT TYPE.**

The 2018 FRG source studies gravitational effective actions depending on the Ricci scalar, Ricci-tensor invariants and products thereof. Functional RG flow determines couplings and a fixed-point effective action **as a function of background Ricci curvature**.

This curvature variable is part of the background-field/EAA representation and truncation/effective-action evaluation. It is not defined through geodesic-sphere distance measurements on an ensemble of quantum geometries.

The 2022 source studies a `k`-dependent self-consistent de-Sitter mean/effective geometry. Its metric spectrum and curvature are properties of a family of effective backgrounds along an RG trajectory. Again, the evaluation procedure is EAA/self-consistent-background based, not a CDT-style quantum-Ricci measurement.
