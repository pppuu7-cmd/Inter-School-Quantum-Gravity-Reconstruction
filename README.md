## Objective

ISQGR is an independent reconstruction programme for quantum gravity. It does **not** begin by selecting a preferred school and does **not** mechanically combine existing theories. Its purpose is to identify whether distinct quantum-gravity programmes contain mutually compatible mechanisms separated by recurrent interface failures, and whether a smaller parent structure can generate the successful mechanisms without inheriting their incompatible assumptions.

The working question is:

> Can a minimal bridge principle explain why several otherwise different quantum-gravity approaches recover overlapping successful structures while failing or remaining incomplete at different interfaces?

A successful outcome may be a candidate model, a sharply constrained equivalence class, or a null result showing that no nontrivial synthesis is justified by the evidence.

## Separation from the existing programmes

- **RQIR Core v1.0** is a frozen model-independent judge of relativity/quantum-interface observables and consistency requirements.
- **KMQGB** is the independent known-model proving ground. `BLOCKED` is not exclusion evidence, and `NEW_REQUIRED` is not assumed here.
- **QGR** constructs an independent candidate from reconstruction constraints.
- **ISQGR** searches the interfaces *between* schools for recurrent structural mismatches and candidate parent principles.

ISQGR must not tune RQIR/KMQGB gates and must not copy the active QGR candidate into the synthesis search. Convergence with QGR is scientifically interesting only if it is obtained independently.

## Non-negotiable rule: mechanisms, not slogans

Broad labels such as *string theory*, *LQG*, *holography*, *causal sets* or *asymptotic safety* are not benchmark objects by themselves. Every promoted mechanism must be tied to a concrete realization, equation, amplitude, action, state space, RG construction, observable map, or source-defined result.

School-level rows are allowed only as navigation/indexing devices and are marked `FAMILY_LEVEL / NOT_BENCHMARK_EVIDENCE` until grounded in concrete realizations.

## Interface-failure principle

An `INTERFACE_FAILURE` is not a declaration that a school is wrong. It is a precisely scoped failure, missing map, incompatibility, or unresolved transition between two required structures, for example:

`microscopic state -> composition law -> continuum geometry -> GR regime -> normalized observable`.

Repeated failures across independent realizations are candidate evidence for a missing bridge principle only after provenance and non-independence are audited.

## Candidate-generation rule

A bridge principle may enter candidate construction only if it:

1. resolves at least two independent interface tensions or unifies at least two independently sourced successful mechanisms;
2. reduces rather than increases arbitrary functional freedom;
3. supplies an explicit mathematical object, not only an analogy;
4. preserves same-realization provenance through the proposed chain;
5. has a cheap falsification route;
6. does not rely on a KMQGB `BLOCKED` result as proof of failure of a known framework.
