## Attack 1 — geodesic renormalization can add an arbitrary length scale and therefore any power

Rejected within the frozen massless low-energy EFT. The renormalization scale `mu` enters dimensionless combinations such as `log(mu l)`. Finite geodesic counterterms in the known perturbative construction alter coefficients and logarithms. They do not by themselves supply a new physical length that changes engineering dimension at fixed order in `G`.

A genuinely resummed higher-derivative sector with a new physical pole/mass would fall outside this frozen assumption and must be treated as a separate theory/regime.

## Attack 2 — logarithms can masquerade as an effective `l^-10` over a finite range

Accepted as a finite-window phenomenological possibility, but it does not alter the analytic classification. A function `l^-8 [a+b log l+c log^2 l]` can have a local effective slope different from `-8` over a restricted range. Therefore a lattice fit near `-10` could not by itself prove cancellation of the `G^2` term even if the matched observable were available.

This strengthens the need for a full functional comparison rather than exponent-only matching.

## Attack 3 — curvature dimensions can acquire an anomalous dimension in quantum gravity

Not within the frozen low-energy perturbative EFT statement. The local curvature operator may require renormalization/mixing, but the separated long-distance nonanalytic term is organized by canonical EFT dimensions and powers of `G`; anomalous/logarithmic dependence appears through logarithms. A genuine fixed-point anomalous scaling regime would be a different UV question.

## Attack 4 — a different relational completion can cancel the master-coordinate `G^2` term

Cannot be rejected from current authority. The known fixed-geodesic construction changes the nonlocal operator and introduces new line-supported contributions/counterterms. No frozen theorem relates its complete curvature coefficient to the master-coordinate coefficient. This is precisely why the result remains conditional.

## Attack 5 — positivity forbids cancellation

Not source-established. Scalar curvature is not a positive operator, the connected correlator need not be positive pointwise, and relational completions include nonlocal correction terms with signs not fixed by simple positivity. No spectral-density proof in the frozen stack protects the coefficient.

## Attack 6 — the EDT `-10` result itself shows cancellation

Rejected as target fitting. The lattice observable lives at finite regulator and uncertain continuum status, and its fitted slope may also be affected by logarithms, observable differences and fit windows. It cannot determine an uncomputed continuum counterterm/cancellation.
