## Attack 1 — higher cumulants contain more equations than two-point covariance

They contain more statistics, but not necessarily more parameter directions. In the frozen reduced action the complete coupling dependence is the single prefactor `1/(g_k lambda_k)`. All action-generated moments are therefore functions of the same one-dimensional parameter unless another operator or measure dependence is introduced.

This is an identifiability-rank statement, not a Gaussian approximation statement.

## Attack 2 — nonlinear vertices may depend on `g_k` and `lambda_k` separately

After the source-defined dimensionless fixed-volume rescaling, all nonlinear vertices arise from the same functional `I[v_3]` multiplied by `sqrt(V_4)/G_k`. Inserting the self-consistent de-Sitter volume makes this prefactor proportional to `1/(g_k lambda_k)`. The cubic, quartic and higher derivatives inherit the same overall coupling dependence.

Thus nonlinearities of the Einstein-Hilbert minisuperspace functional do not by themselves increase parameter rank.

## Attack 3 — `omega` supplies the missing second direction

`omega` is measured in CDT and is essential to the lattice-to-continuum geometric rescaling, but in the frozen direct comparison it is not a second FRG Einstein-Hilbert running coupling. ITER078 already uses it to translate the deformed CDT volume profile into the round minisuperspace convention.

Counting `omega` again as an independent equation for `lambda_k` would double-count the geometry/convention map unless a new source equation tied it directly to `lambda_k` independently of the fixed-volume map. The frozen stack does not contain such an equation.

## Attack 4 — the functional measure can carry separate coupling dependence

This is the strongest scope limitation. The direct source writes an effective action and treats its fluctuations perturbatively; it does not prove that the full nonperturbative reduced measure contains no additional coupling dependence.

Therefore the no-go is intentionally scoped to **observables generated by the frozen reduced effective action/truncation**. It is not a theorem about the exact full CDT/FRG measure. A separately source-qualified coupling-dependent measure would reopen the question.

This limitation does not invalidate the no-go at the level actually used by ITER078/080.

## Attack 5 — the volume constraint itself depends on `lambda_k`

The self-consistent source relation already inserts the `lambda_k` dependence of the de-Sitter volume before dimensionless rescaling. Once the ensemble is conditioned on fixed `V_4(k)` and variables are normalized by that volume, the remaining action prefactor depends only on the product.

A **variable-volume** ensemble is different and falls outside the frozen theorem; this is precisely why cosmological susceptibility remains a viable successor.

## Attack 6 — use the lattice transfer-matrix `lambda`

Rejected for the same type reason as ITER089. Its fixed-volume/Lagrange-multiplier role is not a source-defined map to FRG `lambda_k`. Introducing it as the missing parameter would evade, not solve, the identifiability problem.

## Attack 7 — higher-curvature corrections change the conclusion

Yes, if they survive as independently renormalized continuum operators. That would enlarge the truncation beyond Einstein-Hilbert and is explicitly outside the no-go. Existing CDT subleading corrections in the frozen source were not source-qualified as such continuum operators.

## Attack 8 — local observables can split the couplings

Potentially. The no-go does not include local/non-minisuperspace sectors. ITER083B and the spectral route show that such sectors bring their own normalization and typing problems, but they are not forbidden by ITER090.

## Verdict

The scoped no-go survives all attacks:

**`PASS_SCOPED_ONE_PARAMETER_FIXED_VOLUME_IDENTIFIABILITY_NOGO`**.

It should be used as a route-selection theorem: stop spending iterations on additional moments/eigenmodes of the same fixed-volume global volume action if the purpose is only to separate `g_k` and `lambda_k` within the two-coupling Einstein-Hilbert minisuperspace truncation.

The cleanest remaining global escape is a variable-total-volume/cosmological-response observable, which changes the ensemble rather than double-counting the fixed-volume distribution.
