## Attack 1 — one cubic interaction plus a nonlinear curvature insertion gives a triangle master

Not after distinguishing loop-dependent from fixed external denominators. In a two-point correlator, one line from the cubic vertex can carry the fixed external momentum while the remaining two internal lines carry `p` and `p+q`. The loop integration is a bubble; the extra fixed propagator belongs to the external/composite kernel.

## Attack 2 — a general covariant graviton gauge can generate more denominator topology

Longitudinal propagator pieces may introduce higher powers such as `1/(p^2)^2`, but they remain in the same two-point bubble family `B_ab` with raised propagator powers and tensor numerators. They do not create a third independent loop momentum denominator. Such powers must be retained during a future gauge check.

## Attack 3 — the one-propagator line self-contraction is a scaleless tadpole and must vanish

Rejected. For two distinct line parameters, the phase `exp[i(tau-sigma)p.l]` supplies a nonzero coordinate separation. The integral is the massless propagator evaluated along the line, not a scaleless momentum tadpole. Its singular region is `tau -> sigma`, where defect renormalization is required.

Only the exact zero-separation/scaleless piece after proper distributional separation may vanish in dimensional regularization.

## Attack 4 — the M/G line phases create an infinite family of new loop masters

They create an infinite-looking set of numerator/weight variants but not an infinite denominator topology. At the frozen order there are at most two line parameters. Tensor numerators can be represented by derivatives with respect to `l`, `q` or parameter variables acting on a small scalar phase-dressed family.

The remaining challenge is reduction of weights/moments, not generation of infinitely many loop momenta.

## Attack 5 — chi2 can contain more than two nested line integrations

At second perturbative order the geodesic solution may contain nested kernels, but each term is bilinear in first-order metric data or one second-order metric contribution. The source fixed-geodesic construction closes at at most two independent affine parameters at this order. Additional nested notation can be reduced by ordered-domain integrals without adding a third independent metric insertion.

## Attack 6 — curvature R3 necessarily adds a three-propagator loop

A Gaussian `R3-R1` contraction leaves a self-contraction/tadpole pair at the composite point, which is scaleless in the frozen massless DR setup, or reduces to fixed external factors plus lower topology when interactions are included. The nonvanishing one-loop two-point core remains bubble-type.

## Attack 7 — counterterm insertions should be merged into the bubble family

For subdivergence graphs they can sit inside a loop and should be reduced with the corresponding bubble/line family. But a tree counterterm insertion multiplied by a running defect coefficient has no loop integration and must remain a separate bookkeeping category; otherwise RG-induced contributions to `B_1` can be double-counted.

## Attack 8 — the source precedent `G_0^2` proves the curvature coefficients

Rejected. It supports the denominator/master topology only. Curvature derivatives, ghosts, composite mixing and genuine defect counterterms change numerators and residues. No numerical coefficient transfers.
