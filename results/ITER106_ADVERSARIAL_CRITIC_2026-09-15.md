Rejected. In a statistically homogeneous fixed geometry, a **normalized conditional pair average** at fixed radius divides by the shell-pair multiplicity, so a pointwise correlator `C(r)~r^-p` remains `~r^-p`; one does not multiply by the shell volume unless using an unnormalized shell integral.
