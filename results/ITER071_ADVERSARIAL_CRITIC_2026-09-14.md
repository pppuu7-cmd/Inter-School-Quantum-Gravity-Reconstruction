## Target

Attempt to falsify or strengthen the researcher classification

`PASS_SCOPED_COMMON_DEFINITION_DIFFERENT_REALIZATION`.

## Attack 1 — The averaging procedures are different, so there is no same observable

CDT estimates return probabilities by random walks on sampled Euclideanized triangulations and ensemble/configuration averaging. QEG defines a metric path-integral expectation and evaluates it using effective scale-dependent metrics.

This difference is real, but it does not destroy definition-level observable identity. In both constructions the reported spectral dimension is the logarithmic slope of a normalized return probability generated by a Laplace/heat operator. The realization and averaging maps differ and must remain typed. Therefore the attack blocks physical-object equality, not the common definition.

**Result: does not force FAIL; reinforces the `DIFFERENT_REALIZATION` qualifier.**

## Attack 2 — QEG `T` is secretly RG time

Rejected by the sources. `T` is fictitious diffusion time. The RG scale enters separately through the effective metric/operator and is associated with Laplacian mode resolution (`k^2 = E_n`, or `k ~ |p|` in the flat reference approximation). The heat kernel then weights these modes as a function of `T`.

**Result: control catches the swap.**

## Attack 3 — CDT diffusion time is secretly proper time

Rejected by the CDT source. The diffusion parameter is fictitious random-walk duration. CDT proper time is a distinct global discrete geometric variable, and the paper explicitly warns that proper time need not coincide with time appearing in physical observables.

**Result: control catches the swap.**

## Attack 4 — The 2011 QEG paper directly compares QEG and CDT, therefore full comparator PASS is mandatory

Too strong for the frozen gate. The detailed comparison in the 2011 paper is carried out for three-dimensional CDT Monte Carlo data from another source. It explicitly states that doing the analogous comparison in four dimensions requires detailed 4D Monte Carlo data. The frozen CDT source in ITER071 is the 2005 4D measurement and does not provide an absolute map from integer diffusion steps to a continuum physical length/RG resolution scale.

The existence of a published 3D comparison is positive evidence that the common definition is operationally meaningful, but it does not satisfy the preregistered 4D scale-map predicate F.

**Result: does not justify `PASS_SCOPED_SAME_OBSERVABLE_COMPARATOR_AUTHORIZED`.**

## Attack 5 — `sqrt(sigma)` is already a physical length map in CDT

On a fixed smooth geometry, the standard heat kernel gives `sqrt(sigma)` as an effective linear diffusion spread. On the CDT lattice, however, `sigma` is an integer random-walk duration in lattice units, and the frozen source retains discretization and finite-size artifacts. Converting that dimensionless diffusion duration into an absolute continuum physical length requires a lattice-spacing/continuum calibration not supplied in the frozen 2005 source.

**Result: insufficient for pointwise physical-scale matching.**

## Attack 6 — Numerical agreement near spectral dimension 2 proves the bridge

Rejected prospectively. A similar short-distance value is compatible with many inequivalent microscopic mechanisms and does not identify their dynamics, measures, state spaces or RG maps. Moreover, the CDT value is an extrapolation from a restricted finite-lattice fit window, while the QEG UV fixed-point value can be an asymptotic theoretical result.

**Result: `NUMERICAL_COINCIDENCE_CONTROL` succeeds.**

## Attack 7 — The QEG result is truncation-free, so scale matching is exact

Only the fixed-point limiting value has the strongest truncation-independent status in the cited analysis. The finite-`T` running curve used for detailed comparisons depends on effective-metric/RG-trajectory input and, in the 2011 treatment away from the fixed point, the Einstein-Hilbert/flat-space approximation. Exactness of an asymptotic limit does not supply the missing CDT physical scale calibration.

**Result: no promotion.**

## Attack 8 — Common heat-kernel language is merely a forgetful algebraic coincidence

This attack is weaker than in ITER068/070. Here the common object is not just generic composition or one-parameter evolution: both sides explicitly define the same measurement functional from return probability to logarithmic spectral dimension, and the QEG source itself frames the generalized `D_s(T)` as a comparator to discrete quantum-gravity approaches.

Still, the common definition does not imply equality of the objects being averaged or of the physical scale maps.

**Result: definition-level commonality survives.**
