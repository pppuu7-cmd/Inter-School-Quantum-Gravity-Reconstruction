## Attack 1 — metric field redefinitions change what 'physical geodesic' means, so the line operators cannot be quotiented

This is the central caveat. A field redefinition cannot be applied to the action alone while keeping a metric-defined observable fixed by notation. ITER120 explicitly transforms the geodesic and endpoint curvature together. Under that **complete** transformation, predictions for the same operational observable are invariant and the two curvature-linear metric-coordinate directions are representation redundancies.

If instead one declares the redefined metric to define a genuinely different operational ruler while leaving the observable prescription unchanged, one has changed the relational observable. That is outside the quotient claim.

## Attack 2 — int R and int R_nn have independent finite counterterms and are therefore physical

Independent coefficients can appear in an intermediate renormalization scheme, but their effect can be redistributed by the two independent local metric redefinition parameters `a,b` together with induced endpoint/action shifts. They are not independent invariant matching data of the consistently transformed observable at this order.

## Attack 3 — higher-derivative local field redefinitions also remove the Box_perp sectors

Not at the frozen order without adding another scale/order. `kappa^2 Box R_mn` is dimensionful and cannot be added to a dimensionless metric with only an `O(kappa^2)` coefficient. The standard local higher-derivative redefinition carrying enough inverse mass dimension starts at higher EFT/gravitational order (schematically `kappa^4 Box R_mn`) or would require an extra physical scale. It therefore does not quotient the `O(kappa^2)` defect directions used here.

## Attack 4 — choose l^2 as the missing scale in the metric field redefinition

Rejected as a **theory** field redefinition. A transformation `delta g ~ kappa^2 l^2 Box R` depends on the separation of the particular observable being evaluated and is therefore an observable-dependent nonlocal field-coordinate change, not a local redefinition of the underlying metric field. Such dependence belongs to defect-operator renormalization, where the Box_perp sectors are already retained.

## Attack 5 — endpoint shift generated by the field redefinition was ignored

Rejected. The source audit explicitly retains the simultaneous endpoint-curvature transformation; around flat space it generates a dimension-4 `Box R`-type local shift. Quotienting only the line term while dropping this compensation would indeed be invalid.

## Attack 6 — the quotient makes the genuine defect rank exactly two physically

Only within the frozen one-loop/local-field-redefinition equivalence class. Higher-order EFT corrections or a different relational observable may enlarge/reorganize the basis. ITER120 is not an all-orders theorem.

## Attack 7 — redundant directions can be discarded diagram by diagram

Rejected. They remain useful and may be necessary intermediate counterterms/gauge checks. Redundancy means the final physical answer is invariant under their consistent redistribution, not that their raw diagrams are zero.
