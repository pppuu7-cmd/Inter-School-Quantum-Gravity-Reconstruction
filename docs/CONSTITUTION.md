# ISQGR Reconstruction Constitution v1.0

Status: **FROZEN FOR PHASE-0/1 METHODOLOGY**

Purpose: prevent school cherry-picking, hindsight synthesis, hidden model splicing, criterion drift, and premature claims of new physics.

## 1. Separation of powers

- RQIR Core v1.0 remains an external frozen judge.
- KMQGB remains the independent known-model/candidate proving ground.
- QGR remains an independent candidate-construction programme.
- ISQGR may use public/source-grounded information about all programmes, but it may not import QGR candidate assumptions as synthesis axioms.

A future convergence between QGR and ISQGR counts only if the relevant structures were reached independently.

## 2. Evidence classes

Every material statement must be tagged with one of:

- `PROVED`
- `NUMERICALLY_VERIFIED`
- `SOURCE_GROUNDED`
- `DERIVED_ISQGR`
- `CONJECTURED`
- `OPEN_BLOCKED`
- `REFUTED_IN_SCOPE`
- `FAMILY_LEVEL_NOT_EVIDENCE`

A school-level summary is never by itself sufficient for a model-level inference.

## 3. Synthesis-state vocabulary

Use only:

- `UNMAPPED`
- `MAPPED`
- `TENSION_CANDIDATE`
- `INTERFACE_FAILURE_SCOPED`
- `BRIDGE_HYPOTHESIS`
- `BRIDGE_DERIVED`
- `CANDIDATE_PARTIAL`
- `CANDIDATE_PROMOTABLE`
- `REJECTED`

`CANDIDATE_PROMOTABLE` means only sufficiently specified for external frozen benchmarking.

## 4. No mechanical hybridization

ISQGR may not construct a theory by simply concatenating ingredients from different schools.

For any imported mechanism A from realization RA and mechanism B from realization RB, at least one of the following must be established before they can coexist in a candidate:

1. both descend from a newly specified common parent object;
2. an explicit functor/map/limit embeds both into one mathematical structure;
3. a controlled effective reduction shows that one emerges from the other;
4. a compatibility theorem establishes a common state, composition and dynamical framework.

Analogy, shared vocabulary, or phenomenological resemblance is insufficient.

## 5. Same-realization obligation

A promoted candidate must eventually support one chain:

`primitive -> states -> dynamics/amplitude -> composition -> coarse graining -> Lorentzian geometry -> GR regime -> matter/source rule -> normalized observable`.

No PASS may be assembled from mutually incompatible realizations.

## 6. Interface-first policy

The programme first maps interfaces; it does not first invent equations.

A candidate bridge principle is admissible only after a recurrent motif is documented across at least two independent source chains or after one unusually sharp incompatibility produces a mathematically forced repair.

## 7. Independence audit

Two apparent confirmations do not count as independent if they share the same derivation, effective action, duality dictionary, numerical ensemble, continuum assumption, or source lineage.

Every recurrent motif must carry a dependence note.

## 8. Freedom tax

Any bridge principle introducing free functions, unconstrained measures, branch weights, regulators, counterterms, or tunable interpolation maps must declare their dimension and physical origin.

A bridge that solves a tension only by adding enough freedom to fit both sides is not a successful synthesis.

## 9. Rigidity preference

Prefer principles forced by:

- composition/gluing consistency;
- causal consistency;
- gauge/relational closure;
- positivity/normalization;
- continuum/RG closure;
- common parameter identity;
- controlled GR and quantum limits.

## 10. Negative evidence discipline

`OPEN_BLOCKED` is never equivalent to `FAIL`.

A missing same-realization object, unavailable source, unresolved normalization, or absent continuum map is a research blocker, not evidence that the parent school is false.

## 11. New-physics threshold

The phrase `NEW_PHYSICS_CANDIDATE` is authorized only if a derived bridge structure:

1. is not merely a renaming of a known mechanism;
2. closes a documented interface failure;
3. produces at least one structural or observable consequence not inserted by definition;
4. survives an explicit nearest-known-framework reduction test;
5. has a falsification or no-go route.

Until then use `BRIDGE_HYPOTHESIS`.

## 12. RQIR mapping discipline

Any candidate is mapped to frozen RQIR Q1–Q7 only after its native objects are specified. RQIR criteria are not modified to accommodate the candidate.

## 13. KMQGB promotion discipline

ISQGR may prepare a candidate record, but independent KMQGB evaluation remains external. A KMQGB `BLOCKED` known-framework lane cannot be used as proof that the ISQGR candidate is required.

## 14. Falsification-first requirement

For each bridge hypothesis record:

- cheapest decisive test;
- assumptions under test;
- kill condition;
- nearest known-framework explanation;
- what survives if the hypothesis fails.

## 15. Claim locks

Until independently established, ISQGR must not claim:

- all existing schools are wrong;
- all schools are mutually inconsistent;
- a new theory is necessary;
- the candidate is unique;
- a family-level blocker is a no-go theorem;
- convergence with QGR proves correctness;
- benchmark incompleteness is evidence for novelty.

## 16. Recovery and provenance

Every active iteration must update `recovery/state.json` and `recovery/CURRENT_FRONT.md`. Source-grounded mechanism records must preserve exact citation/provenance and scope.

## 17. Versioning

This constitution is `v1.0`. Scientific changes require a new version, rationale independent of a desired model outcome, changelog, and re-audit of any bridge hypotheses produced under the prior version.
