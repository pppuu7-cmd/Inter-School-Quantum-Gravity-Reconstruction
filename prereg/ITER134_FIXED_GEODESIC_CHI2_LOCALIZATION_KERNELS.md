# ITER134 — second-order fixed-geodesic localization-kernel prerequisite gate

## Question
Can the second-order straight-background fixed-geodesic/world-function localization object required by frozen ITER132 be represented as an explicit target-blind 4D ordered affine kernel, with its required differentiated variants, without replacing the nonlocal object by a scalar proxy?

## Frozen scope
Work on the Euclidean straight segment `z(tau)=x+tau*l*n`, `tau in [0,1]`. Build a typed second-order localization kernel `chi2` as the sum of (i) a direct quadratic metric insertion on the segment and (ii) an ordered double insertion generated by the first-order geodesic/localization response. Preserve the two affine parameters and ordering domain explicitly. Also generate first momentum derivatives of the kernel needed to represent differentiated localization insertions. Overall convention-dependent world-function/segment normalization may be factored into a single symbolic prefactor, but relative signs, ordering and tensor/momentum dependence are frozen and must be checked.

This gate does **not** evaluate loop integrals, pole residues, B1, EDT exponents, or any cross-school fit.

## Frozen checks
A. D=4 symmetric graviton basis and explicit tensor indices for both metric insertions.
B. `chi2` retains a two-parameter ordered affine representation; no local scalar collapse.
C. Bose exchange of the two graviton legs maps the two ordered domains into the symmetrized full kernel exactly.
D. Endpoint reversal `tau -> 1-tau`, `sigma -> 1-sigma`, momenta reversed, gives the preregistered covariance of the full symmetrized kernel.
E. Soft-leg reduction: sending one graviton momentum to zero reduces to the corresponding first-order line-kernel insertion times the constant-leg tensor factor, up to the single declared overall normalization convention.
F. Momentum differentiation of the exponential/ordered kernel agrees with direct affine-coordinate insertion and has the expected homogeneity/coordinate degree.
G. At least three independent integer/rational held-out momentum/polarization configurations agree between symbolic generated kernels and direct numerical affine integration to exact rational/algebraic tolerance where available, otherwise numerical relative tolerance <= 1e-10.
H. No EDT exponent, B1 value, desired sign, or bridge target is present in generator or tests.

## Classification
