# ITER090 preregistration — fixed-volume Einstein-Hilbert minisuperspace identifiability no-go

Date: 2026-09-15
Gate: `ITER090_FIXED_VOLUME_EH_MINISUPERSPACE_IDENTIFIABILITY_NOGO`

## Motivation frozen before derivation

ITER080 found that the direct reduced CDT↔FRG map constrains the product `g_k lambda_k` but not the two couplings separately. ITER089 showed that the Gaussian covariance/Hessian of the same global volume variable is not an independent second observable.

Before searching higher global cumulants, ITER090 tests whether this is a structural identifiability obstruction of the entire fixed-four-volume Einstein-Hilbert minisuperspace truncation.

## Frozen source

Primary: arXiv `2408.07808`, Sec. II, especially the self-consistent de-Sitter volume, fixed-volume minisuperspace action and effective fluctuation coupling.

Corroborating: arXiv `1510.08719`, only for the fact that CDT global volume fluctuations are described by the reduced effective action and its covariance/transfer-matrix reconstruction.

## Frozen question

After imposing the source-defined self-consistent de-Sitter background and rescaling to dimensionless fixed-volume variables, does the Einstein-Hilbert minisuperspace action depend on the FRG couplings `g_k` and `lambda_k` only through their product?

If yes, then within this truncation all action-generated fixed-volume correlation functions/cumulants of the same dimensionless scale-factor/three-volume variable are one-parameter functions and cannot identify `g_k` and `lambda_k` separately without an additional observable or assumption.

## Required predicates

A. Source-defined `G_k=g_k/k^2` and `Lambda_k=lambda_k k^2`.

B. Source-defined self-consistent four-sphere volume `V_4(k)` as a function of `lambda_k` and `k`.

C. Source-defined fixed-volume minisuperspace action after dimensionless rescaling, including its complete coupling-dependent prefactor at Einstein-Hilbert level.

D. Exact algebraic elimination of `k`, showing whether the prefactor is a function of `g_k lambda_k` only.

E. The claim is restricted to observables generated by this fixed-volume reduced action; measure effects or operators not contained in the truncation cannot be silently included.

F. The CDT deformation/profile parameter `omega` is kept as a lattice-to-round-geometry rescaling already used by the direct map, not miscounted as a second FRG Einstein-Hilbert coupling.

G. A higher cumulant counts as independent only if its source-level expression contains coupling dependence beyond the single effective prefactor.

H. Leaving fixed `V_4`, adding higher-derivative operators, using local observables or changing truncation is explicitly outside the no-go scope.

## Frozen controls

- `CUMULANT_COUNTING_CONTROL`: infinitely many cumulants do not imply infinitely many coupling directions if generated by a one-parameter probability family.
- `MEASURE_ASSUMPTION_CONTROL`: the no-go applies to the coupling dependence represented by the frozen reduced effective action; unknown coupling-dependent functional-measure effects are not claimed absent from full quantum gravity.
- `OMEGA_SECOND_FRG_COUPLING_CONTROL`: `omega` cannot be counted as a separate FRG EH coupling after it has already been used to map anisotropic CDT conventions to the round minisuperspace form.
- `FIXED_VARIABLE_VOLUME_SWAP_CONTROL`: grand-canonical/variable-volume susceptibilities are outside the theorem and may remain candidates.
- `EH_HIGHER_OPERATOR_CONTROL`: a higher-curvature coupling would enlarge theory space and is outside the two-coupling EH no-go.
- `TRUNCATION_FULL_THEORY_CONTROL`: no conclusion about full FRG/CDT identifiability follows beyond the frozen truncation.

## Frozen classifications

- `PASS_SCOPED_ONE_PARAMETER_FIXED_VOLUME_IDENTIFIABILITY_NOGO` if the exact reduced action contains `g_k,lambda_k` only through `g_k lambda_k` after source-defined rescaling.
- `FAIL_SCOPED_ADDITIONAL_EH_COUPLING_DEPENDENCE_SURVIVES` if a second independent dependence on `g_k` or `lambda_k` remains in the frozen action.
- `BLOCKED_SOURCE_AUTHORITY` if the source formulas are insufficient for exact algebraic adjudication.

## Claim ceiling

A PASS is a no-go only for separating the two Einstein-Hilbert couplings using observables generated by the same fixed-volume minisuperspace action. It does not forbid variable-volume, local, non-minisuperspace or higher-operator observables; it does not reject asymptotic safety, create bridge credit or authorize a candidate theory.

Predicates, controls and classifications are frozen before algebraic adjudication.
