## Frozen outputs

1. explicit symbolic or sparse-polynomial numerator representation in q and p;
2. total q-degree and p-degree bounds;

5. at least two held-out integer external-momentum evaluations independently recomputed from direct tensor contraction;
6. a manifest record of zero versus nonzero numerator before integration.
