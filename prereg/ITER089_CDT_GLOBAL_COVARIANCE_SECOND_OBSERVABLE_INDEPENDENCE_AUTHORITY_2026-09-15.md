# ITER089 preregistration — CDT global volume-covariance as an independent second observable

Date: 2026-09-15
Gate: `ITER089_CDT_GLOBAL_COVARIANCE_SECOND_OBSERVABLE_INDEPENDENCE_AUTHORITY`

## Motivation frozen before adjudication

ITER088 closes the route that tried to use the first nonconstant spatial Laplace mode as the second observable complementing the direct CDT↔FRG reduced map. The direct 2024 source itself states that global quantities such as the three-volume receive contributions from constant spatial modes, so the next candidate must remain in the global/minisuperspace sector.

The most obvious candidate is the spectrum/shape of the covariance matrix of `N_3(t)` fluctuations. However, those fluctuations were historically used to reconstruct the same effective minisuperspace action from which the parameters `Gamma` and `omega` entering ITER078 were obtained.

ITER089 therefore asks whether covariance/Hessian information contains a genuinely independent coupling-sensitive observable, or whether it is algebraically/statistically redundant with the already-used reduced-action parameters.

## Frozen sources

- arXiv `1510.08719` — full covariance-matrix reconstruction of the four-dimensional CDT effective action and transfer-matrix cross-check.
- arXiv `1403.5940` — transfer-matrix effective action in the de-Sitter phase.
- arXiv `2408.07808` — direct CDT↔FRG reduced action and constant-mode/minisuperspace interpretation.
- ITER078 terminal/source records — only for the existing parameter map; no new independence may be inferred from them.

## Frozen question

After fixing the mean `N_3(t)` profile and the parameters already used by ITER078 (`Gamma`, `omega`, volume convention), does the measured covariance/Hessian contain at least one source-qualified large-volume quantity whose continuum interpretation is independent enough to constrain `g_k` and `lambda_k` separately?

Candidates may include:

- a Hessian eigenvalue ratio not fixed by the known minisuperspace action;
- a transfer-matrix potential coefficient with a source-defined continuum meaning independent of `omega` and volume fixing;
- a stable subleading/higher-curvature coefficient that survives the large-volume/continuum interpretation.

## Required predicates

A. The covariance matrix `C_tt'` and inverse fluctuation matrix `P=C^{-1}` are explicitly related to the second variation of the effective action.

B. The leading large-volume effective action and its independent parameters are explicit.

C. It is determined whether the full leading Hessian spectrum is fixed once the background profile plus `Gamma`/shape/volume parameters are fixed.

D. Any candidate extra parameter must have a large-volume source-defined continuum interpretation rather than being a volume-fixing Lagrange multiplier, discretization offset, or finite-size correction.

E. A transfer-matrix parameter named `lambda` cannot be identified with the FRG dimensionless cosmological coupling `lambda_k` merely by name; its role in the fixed-volume lattice effective action must be source-qualified.

F. A subleading kinetic or curvature correction may count only if the source shows it is stable against volume/discretization changes and interpretable as a continuum coupling.

G. If covariance eigenmodes are merely predictions/checks of the already fitted reduced action, they receive consistency credit but no independent-observable credit.

H. No FRG trajectory or held-out CDT observable may be used to define independence post hoc.

## Frozen controls

- `COVARIANCE_PARAMETER_DOUBLE_COUNT_CONTROL`: the data used to fit `Gamma`/shape cannot be counted again as an independent constraint without an orthogonal statistic or held-out mode.
- `LATTICE_LAMBDA_FRG_LAMBDA_NAME_CONTROL`: lattice/volume-fixing `lambda` and FRG `lambda_k` remain distinct unless a source-derived map exists.
- `SUBLEADING_CONTINUUM_CONTROL`: finite-size/discretization corrections cannot become new continuum couplings by relabeling.
- `TRANSFER_COVARIANCE_METHOD_CONTROL`: agreement of transfer-matrix and covariance methods validates reconstruction but does not create a second physical observable if both fit the same effective action.
- `ZERO_MODE_CONSTRAINT_CONTROL`: artificial zero modes generated by fixed-volume constraints cannot be interpreted as physical massless modes.
- `BACKGROUND_SHAPE_CONTROL`: Hessian shape determined by the mean de-Sitter profile is not independent of `omega` if the source equations fix it.

## Frozen classifications

- `PASS_SCOPED_INDEPENDENT_GLOBAL_SECOND_OBSERVABLE_IDENTIFIED` if a large-volume continuum-qualified covariance/Hessian statistic exists beyond the already-used reduced-action parameters.
- `FAIL_SCOPED_COVARIANCE_REDUNDANT_WITH_REDUCED_ACTION_PARAMETERS` if the leading covariance/Hessian is completely the second variation of the same fitted action and no stable extra continuum parameter survives.
- `PASS_SCOPED_SUBLEADING_CANDIDATE_CONTINUUM_AUTHORITY_OPEN` if an extra measured coefficient exists but its continuum versus discretization status remains unresolved.
- `BLOCKED_SOURCE_AUTHORITY` if the frozen stack does not expose the needed parameter dependence.

## Claim ceiling

A PASS can at most authorize a new global observable for a later cross-framework gate. It cannot by itself split FRG couplings, select a trajectory, establish a fixed point, create bridge credit or authorize candidate-theory construction.

Predicates, controls and classifications are frozen before terminal adjudication.
