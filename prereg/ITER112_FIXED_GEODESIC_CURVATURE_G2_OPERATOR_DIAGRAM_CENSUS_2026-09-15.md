# ITER112 preregistration — fixed-geodesic scalar-curvature `O(G^2)` operator / diagram census

Date: 2026-09-15
Gate: `ITER112_FIXED_GEODESIC_CURVATURE_G2_OPERATOR_DIAGRAM_CENSUS`

## Motivation frozen before cancellation analysis

ITER111 saturated the public-source route for an already-computed four-dimensional perturbative scalar-curvature correlator at fixed fluctuating geodesic distance. ITER109 showed that any nonzero separated `O(G^2)` term must have engineering form `G^2/l^8` up to logarithms, but ITER110 showed that the coefficient is not protected across different nonlocal relational completions.

Before attempting loop integration, ITER112 asks whether the missing calculation has a finite, source-controlled perturbative census and exactly which classes can participate in an `O(G^2)` cancellation.

1. arXiv:1706.01891 / CQG 35, 035005 (2018) — explicit fixed-geodesic perturbative expansion and classification into field-theoretic, mixed geodesic/field, and pure geodesic corrections.
2. arXiv:2510.11888 / PRD 113, 106032 (2026) — explicit perturbative curvature insertions and one-loop master-coordinate curvature correlator.

## Frozen setup

Use flat Euclidean background with

`g_mn = delta_mn + kappa h_mn`, `kappa^2 = 16 pi G`,

and scalar curvature expansion

`R = kappa R_1 + kappa^2 R_2 + kappa^3 R_3 + ...`.

For a fixed-length geodesic endpoint,

`chi = chi_0 + kappa chi_1 + kappa^2 chi_2 + ...`,

so the endpoint curvature insertion must be Taylor-expanded consistently in both the curvature field and geodesic embedding.

ITER112 is a census/closure gate. It does **not** claim to evaluate the final coefficient.

## Required predicates

A. Show that all separated contributions through `O(kappa^4)=O(G^2)` can be organized into a finite set of operator/diagram classes.

B. Keep ordinary curvature-field corrections separate from geodesic-localization corrections.

C. Include first-order mixed localization terms generated by `chi_1` and second-order pure localization terms generated by `chi_2` and `chi_1 chi_1`.

D. Include curvature-composite renormalization / higher-curvature counterterms and geodesic-embedding renormalization as distinct sectors.

E. Identify which classes can contribute noncontact terms and therefore can participate in cancellation of the `G^2/l^8` coefficient; pure contact terms cannot be used to cancel a separated tail.

F. Gauge-parameter cancellation must be a property of the complete fixed-geodesic observable, not of any single class.

G. No modern EDT exponent or fit may be used to omit/include classes or choose finite counterterms.

H. A complete PASS requires a finite calculation graph with no missing source-required class at `O(G^2)`; it does not require evaluating the integrals.

## Claim ceiling

A PASS only establishes that the original `O(G^2)` calculation is well-posed with a finite census. It cannot determine the coefficient, prove cancellation/noncancellation, establish a direct EDT/EFT conflict, continuum EDT, `BRIDGE_DERIVED`, new physics or a candidate theory.
